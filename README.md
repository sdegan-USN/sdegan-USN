# Sean Egan

I lead people, and I build AI systems with coding agents.

I lead The Informed, a volunteer group of scientists and service members who build tools for
Navy meteorology, oceanography, and space, and give them away. I'm a Navy Information Warfare
Officer (Reserve). I hold a PhD in atmospheric chemistry.
